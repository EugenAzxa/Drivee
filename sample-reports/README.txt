Drivee — Sample community-report photos
========================================

Drop FOUR photos in this folder with these EXACT names (lowercase, .jpg):

  pothole.jpg        A pothole in the road
  meter.jpg          A broken / out-of-order parking meter or pay machine
  road-damage.jpg    Cracked or damaged pavement
  faded-sign.jpg     A faded or hard-to-read parking sign

Guidelines:
- Use your own photos or royalty-free images (you own the rights).
- Roughly square or landscape, around 800px wide is plenty. Keep each under ~300 KB so the app stays fast.
- The app already references these paths. As soon as the files are here and deployed,
  they appear automatically in the map pin popups and the reports list.
- If a file is missing, the app just hides the image and still shows the pin + text,
  so nothing breaks while you gather the photos.

These appear as clearly-labelled "SAMPLE" examples so real users are never misled
into thinking they are live community reports.
